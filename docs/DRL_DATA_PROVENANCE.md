# Data Provenance and Dataset Audit for AuraTour DRL Integration

## 1. Overview and Provenance Taxonomy

Every data element and feature utilized in the AuraTour Deep Reinforcement Learning pipeline is audited and classified according to the 7-tier provenance taxonomy:

| Classification | Definition | Application in AuraTour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`observed`** | Directly measured or recorded by official turnstiles or geographic surveys. | Monument GPS coordinates (OSM), Railway station coordinates (GPKG). |
| **`sourced`** | Published in official records or secondary repositories, but not a continuous turnstile log. | ASI annual visitor totals, Kaggle ticket prices, visit durations. |
| **`calculated`** | Derived mathematically from observed/sourced coordinates using verified formulas. | Haversine distances, Great-circle bearings. |
| **`estimated`** | Derived using empirical factors or models from national agencies. | Road transit distances ($1.30\times$ detour factor), modal transit emissions (WRI India). |
| **`heuristic`** | Approximated using domain literature rules. | Regional seasonal tourism demand index, taxi transit speed (30 km/h in city). |
| **`simulated`** | Generated by the MDP environment during RL exploration and transition steps. | Sequential visit trajectories, intermediate battery/budget decrements. |
| **`unknown`** | Provenance unverified or untraceable. | None permitted in production. |

## 2. Primary Data Sources

### Source 1: Kaggle Indian Tourist Places Dataset
- **Raw File:** `kaggle_indian_tourist_places_dataset.zip` / `indian_tourist_places_dataset.csv`
- **Volume:** 13,000 records across 12 Indian states.
- **Fields:** State, City, Name, Type, User Rating, Number of Reviews, Best Time to Visit, Entry Fee (INR), Visit Duration (Hours), Recommended Visit Days, Crowd Level.
- **Extraction & Cleaning Script:** `backend/app/data_ingestion/monument_matcher.py`
- **Processed Artifact:** `backend/data/processed/cleaned_tourist_places_rajasthan.csv` (1,225 matched Rajasthan records).
- **Provenance:** `sourced`. Provides authentic monument entry fees (e.g. ₹50–₹500 for Indian nationals) and authentic visit durations (1.5h to 3.5h).
- **Limitation:** Does not contain longitudinal traveler trajectories or visitor turnstile counts. Single-observation cross-sectional records only.

### Source 2: Central Zone Railway & Bus Infrastructure GeoPackage
- **Raw File:** `central-zone-261022-free.gpkg/central-zone.gpkg` (1.81 GB SQLite GeoPackage).
- **Bounding Box:** $[74.22^\circ\text{ E}, 17.78^\circ\text{ N}]$ to $[84.58^\circ\text{ E}, 31.36^\circ\text{ N}]$ (covers MP, UP, and eastern Rajasthan).
- **Layers:** `lines` (OpenStreetMap railway geometries), `points` (1,875 railway stations, 184 bus terminals).
- **Extraction Script:** `backend/app/data_ingestion/monument_matcher.py`
- **Processed Artifact:** `backend/data/processed/central_zone_stations.csv` (2,563 regional transit hubs).
- **Provenance:** `observed`. Used for multimodal transport network geometry verification.

### Source 3: Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) & Ministry of Tourism Statistics
- **Source:** Dataful.in / Ministry of Tourism Central Protected Ticketed Monuments.
- **Fields:** Fiscal Year, ASI Circle (Jaipur Circle), Monument Name, Domestic Visitors, Foreign Visitors, Total Revenue.
- **Processed Artifact:** `backend/data/processed/monument_matching_registry.csv`
- **Provenance:** `sourced`. Provides annual macro-popularity indicators for major monuments (e.g. Amber Palace ~1.4M–1.8M annual visitors, Chittorgarh ~350k).
- **Limitation:** Aggregated annually; cannot be broken down into daily or hourly crowd counts without fabricated assumptions.

### Source 4: OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Wikidata
- **Coverage:** 45 curated Rajasthan attractions across 18 districts.
- **Fields:** Unique ID, Canonical Name, City, District, Latitude, Longitude, Heritage Status (UNESCO/ASI), Categories.
- **Processed Artifact:** `backend/data/processed/attractions.csv`
- **Provenance:** `observed` coordinates and administrative metadata.

### Source 5: WRI India / India GHG Program
- **Modal Emission Factors:** Train ($17 \text{ g CO}_2\text{e/pkm}$), Bus ($40 \text{ g CO}_2\text{e/pkm}$), Car ($150 \text{ g CO}_2\text{e/pkm}$), Domestic Flight ($130 \text{ g CO}_2\text{e/pkm}$).
- **Provenance:** `estimated` national benchmarks for Indian transport networks.

## 3. Monument Matching Pipeline and Audit

To reconcile the secondary datasets against the 45 canonical Rajasthan attractions, `backend/app/data_ingestion/monument_matcher.py` implements a 4-stage matching hierarchy:

```
Raw Monument Name (e.g. "Amber Fort", "City Palace", "Jantar Mantar")
                    │
                    ▼
Stage 1: Punctuation & Alphanumeric Normalization (lowercase, strip diacritics)
                    │
                    ▼
Stage 2: City-Disambiguation (e.g. "City Palace Jaipur" [raj_003] vs "City Palace Udaipur" [raj_015])
                    │
                    ▼
Stage 3: Verified Alias Table Lookup (e.g. "Amber Fort" -> Amber Palace [raj_001])
                    │
                    ▼
Stage 4: SequenceMatcher Fuzzy Ratio (Threshold: 0.65 for candidate review)
```

### Matching Registry Output (`backend/data/processed/monument_matching_registry.csv`)
- **Total Unique Place Names Audited:** 74 records.
- **Exact & Verified Alias Matches:** 41 records (Confidence 0.95–1.00, Status: `accepted`).
- **Fuzzy Matches:** 12 records (Confidence 0.65–0.90, Status: `accepted` or `needs_review`).
- **Unmatched Records:** 21 records (Confidence $< 0.60$, Status: `unmatched`).
- **Unmatched Treatment:** Retained in audit registry; never discarded or silently coerced into false associations.
